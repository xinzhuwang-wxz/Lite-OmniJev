Cohen κ needs two identifiable paired label streams. Three-way/count data can support a different statistic such as Fleiss κ under its assumptions; it is not the frozen paired Cohen result. Majority agreement, unanimous-only filtering, model/human accuracy, and published dataset-level agreement on another task/subset do not establish κ≥0.7 on these project pools. No qualifying published statistic was established in this bounded review; absence of evidence is not a claim that no unpublished records exist.

Smallest completion path: an **external custodian/gate operator** first verifies existing worker-level records, licenses, exact task and pool-family exclusions without sending payloads to agents. Clotho is worth this one bounded check; A-OKVQA requires genuinely matching MC records, not a task substitution. If records are unavailable/incompatible, the unchanged implemented contract requires two real independent people to annotate 6,000 items (4×1,500), hence 12,000 raw decisions before adjudication, in an externally controlled environment. Existing staff/volunteers may suffice; no hiring or payment is presumed. Task/primitive distribution must remain the original distribution, and screenshot/crossmodal coverage must be reconciled explicitly rather than dropped. Operator returns only counts, κ, hashes, failure categories and signed provenance. User/resource owner must supply that human/isolation resource if unavailable; agents can prepare mechanics but cannot impersonate people or attest their own root host as independent. This remains independent of download readiness and must not be hidden by an “offline prep complete” claim.
